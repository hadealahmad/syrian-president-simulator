# 03. Governance, Institutions & Decrees Design

## 1. The Executive Presidency (Direct Presidential System)

Under the transitional constitutional framework and decrees enacted starting March 13, 2025, executive authority is concentrated in the **President of the Syrian Republic (Ahmed al-Sharaa)**. 

### Key Structural Milestones:
1. **Abolition of Prime Minister Post (March 13, 2025):** Executive authority transformed into a Direct Presidential System where all ministries and sovereign agencies answer directly to the President.
2. **Cabinet Consolidation (March 29, 2025):** Consolidation of scattered ministries into strategic portfolios (Energy, Economy & Industry, Emergency & Disasters).
3. **May 9, 2026 Presidential Reshuffle (Decree 98 / المرسوم 98):**
   - Agriculture Portfolio assigned to **Basil Hafez al-Suwaidan** (replacing Amjad Badr).
   - Information Portfolio assigned to **Khaled Fawaz Zaarour** (replacing Hamza al-Mustafa).
   - General Secretariat of the Presidency assigned to **Abdulrahman al-Aama** (replacing Maher al-Sharaa).

### The Political Capital (PC) Economy
Political Capital represents the president's political leverage, popular legitimacy, and moral authority to enforce compliance across ministries, provincial councils, and armed factions.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       POLITICAL CAPITAL (PC) ENGINE                         │
├──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SOURCES OF INFLOW:                   │ USES & EXPENDITURE VECTORS:          │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ * High Civic Trust (> 60)            │ * Enacting Executive Decrees (15-40) │
│ * Successful Turn Surpluses          │ * Ordering Port & Customs Audits (20)│
│ * International Aid Agreements       │ * Purging Corrupt Oligarchs (35-50)  │
│ * Restoring Grid Power to Cities     │ * Overriding Ministerial Vetoes (15) │

```

* PC is capped at **100**.
* If PC falls below **15**, administrative resistance spikes, provincial councils ignore directives, and security commands hesitate to enforce the law.

### Sovereign Body 1: General Secretariat of the Presidency (الأمانة العامة لرئاسة الجمهورية)

### Sovereign Body 2: Central Commission for Inspection and Oversight (الهيئة المركزية للرقابة والتفتيش)
* **Leadership:** Amer Namees al-Ali (appointed May 4, 2025).

## 4. Deep Administrative Dilemmas

### 4.1 The Public Sector Employment & Clientelism Trap
The state inherited 1.1 million civil servants, many redundant or under-qualified.
* Mass dismissals save SYP outlays but generate instantaneous social revolt and street unrest in vulnerable governorates.
* Indiscriminate wage bumps via seigniorage trigger severe parallel exchange market plunges and accelerate domestic inflation.
* The sustainable path mandates selective retention of engineers, doctors, and logistics directors while transitioning clerical personnel to reconstruction cooperatives.

### 4.2 The Competence & Human Capital Vacuum
* Replacing experienced technical personnel with factional partisans triggers catastrophic equipment failures and drops ministry competence below 35%.
* Re-attracting Syrian diaspora experts requires hard USD compensation, causing internal friction with local staff but boosting project execution rates by up to 40%.

## 5. Confiscated Assets & Oligarch Monopolies Engine (Implemented)

### 5.1 Decree 13 & 16 Oligarch Monopolies Pipeline
The Presidential Administration exercises direct jurisdiction over 4 prime oligarch conglomerates seized after the transition:
1. **Syriatel & MTN Telecom:** Mobile carrier duopoly ($650M valuation).
2. **Hamsho Adra Steel Mills:** Metallurgical and scrap recycling monopoly ($280M valuation).
3. **Marota City Real Estate:** High-end Damascus master development ($400M valuation).
4. **Four Seasons Damascus Stake:** 51% luxury hospitality asset ($140M valuation).

### 5.2 Three Divergent Processing Policies
- **80/20 Voluntary Disclosure & Settlement:** Oligarch deposits $350M USD + 4T SYP cash into the state treasury in exchange for retaining corporate management. Yields immediate liquidity with minimal administrative disruption; slight civic trust friction.
- **State Nationalization (SOE):** Total conversion into public sector enterprises. Generates predictable recurring semiannual SYP revenues (up to 600B SYP/turn), but increases systemic corruption (+6%) and bureaucratic inertia.
- **Foreign Liquidation Auction:** Auctioning assets to international or diaspora investors for immediate maximum hard USD liquidity (up to $520M USD), forfeiting future domestic dividends.

### 5.3 Property Restitution vs. Land Monetization (Decree 16)
- **Restitute to Refugees:** Returns properties to original displaced owners. Generates $+12\%$ Civic Trust and accelerates diaspora return, but forfeits immediate asset sales.
- **Monetize as State Land:** Auctions prime tracts to private developers for $\$120\text{M}$ USD and $800\text{B}$ SYP cash, but inflicts severe civic trust backlash ($-18\%$) and inflames IDP grievances.
