## Decree 59 of 2026 — the reconstruction committee

**Issued 8 March 2026, published 10 March 2026 (SANA).** The full Arabic text is
archived by Al-Masaa Al-Suria.

**Article 1 — composition.** A committee chaired by the **Minister of Emergency
and Disaster Management** with membership of:

- Minister of Finance
- Minister of Public Works and Housing
- Minister of Social Affairs and Labour
- Minister of Local Administration and Environment
- **Governors of Aleppo, Hama and Idlib**
- Director of International Cooperation, Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates

**Article 2 — two tasks.** (1) Prepare basic infrastructure in destroyed areas so
residents can return and live in decent conditions. (2) Take all possible
measures to improve services for citizens currently in internal displacement
areas, and support them so as to ease the effects of displacement.

**Article 3** — the committee meets every fifteen days, or when its chair
convenes it, and may call on whomever it judges necessary.

**Article 4** — periodic **monthly reports to the Presidency** on results.

**Operational detail.** Chair **Raed al-Saleh** states the aim is "a Syria
without camps". Two subcommittees sit beneath it: a **financial** one for
budgets and an **executive** one including ministries and governorates not on
the main committee, responsible for fieldwork and coordination. The committee
coordinates with international organisations and is developing a general vision
and timetable.

**Context.** Northern Syria has around **800 displacement camps holding more than
120,000 people**, concentrated in Idlib. The decree followed criticism over camp
conditions and declining donor support.

### Verdict

**VERIFIED, and the fact-check report was correct.** Every element of row 2.7
checks out: the number, the March 2026 date, the chairmanship by the emergency
minister, the membership (Finance, Housing, Social Affairs, Local Administration,
and the governors of Aleppo, Hama and Idlib), and the purpose — infrastructure
rehabilitation in preparation for the return of displaced people and refugees.

The only nuance is that the decree is *dated* 8 March and *published* 10 March;
the report cites the publication date.

### Game impact

**MISSING FROM GAME: the reconstruction committee.** This is the body that would
coordinate the $216B reconstruction programme, and it has a structure the engine
could model almost directly:

- **A 15-day meeting cadence and a monthly report to the Presidency** is a
  bureaucratic-clock mechanic the game has no equivalent for.
- **A budget subcommittee** is a second, distinct fiscal channel separate from
  the treasury — money that bypasses the normal budget and cannot be
  reprioritised by a finance minister.
- **Governor-level seats for Aleppo, Hama and Idlib specifically** concentrate
  the three governorates hosting the most camps, which is a distributional
  tension the game could exploit.
- **Mandate covering both returnees and current IDPs** is a two-audience problem:
  money spent on returning families is money not spent on the still-displaced.
- **Raed al-Saleh already exists in the game** as Minister of Emergency and
  Disaster Management, so the commission could slot into `BASELINE_COMMISSIONS`
  with an existing figure.

**MISSING: camp closure as a policy.** "A Syria without camps" is a legible
objective with obvious costs, and closure is gated on infrastructure
rehabilitation.

## Decrees 98, 100 and 101 of 2026 — the May reshuffle

All three dated **9 May 2026**. Arabic texts archived by Syrian Memory.

- **Decree 98** — `يُعين السيد عبد الرحمن بدر الدين الأعما أميناً عاماً لرئاسة
  الجمهورية`: **Abdulrahman Badr al-Deen al-A'ma** as **Secretary-General of the
  Presidency**. Former **Governor of Homs**, former Minister of Development and
  Humanitarian Affairs. He replaced **Maher al-Sharaa**, the president's brother;
  Al Jazeera reads the move as a response to nepotism criticism, while noting
  the new appointees "come overwhelmingly from his trusted network".
- **Decree 100** — `يُعين السيد خالد فواز زعرور وزيراً للإعلام`: **Khaled Fawaz
  Zaarour** as **Minister of Information**, replacing Hamza Mustafa, who moved to
  Foreign Affairs. Dean of the Faculty of Media at Damascus University from
  March 2025.
- **Decree 101** — `يُعين السيد باسل حافظ السودان وزيراً للزراعة`: **Basel Hafez
  al-Suwaidan** as **Minister of Agriculture**, previously Deputy Minister.
  Al Jazeera notes he is the defence minister's cousin.

The same night issued **gubernatorial appointments for Homs, Quneitra, Latakia and
Deir ez-Zor** (Decree 105 appointed Ziad Fawaz al-Ayash to Deir ez-Zor). Al
Jazeera headlined it as "**Syria's first government reshuffle since al-Assad's
ouster**".

### Verdict

**VERIFIED, and the game is correct on all three**, including the spelling of
al-Suwaidan's name.

### The detail the game does not model

**al-Suwaidan — Agriculture Minister by Decree 101 — is the same person running the
Anti-Illicit Enrichment Commission's settlements.** Enab Baladi: "Head of Syria's
Anti-Illicit Enrichment Commission Basel Hafez al-Suwaidan announces a six month
voluntary disclosure program, January 28, 2026." Al Jazeera describes the
agriculture minister as heading "a committee tasked with reaching settlements
with business tycoons linked to the Assad-era elite".

The settlements programme:
- Six-month **voluntary disclosure window**, announced **28 January 2026**,
  expiring **end of May 2026**.
- **50 settlement requests** to be decided by end of May 2026.
- **Security and army personnel of the ousted regime are not eligible**; the
  commission only prepares indictment files, requests precautionary measures
  against their and their families' property, and refers them to the judiciary.
- Settled properties may be disclosed under a transparency principle.
- After the window, all files go **exclusively** to the judiciary.

**MISSING FROM GAME:** agriculture policy and asset recovery concentrated in one
pair of hands, with a live six-month settlement deadline. The game separates
agriculture into a ministry and confiscation into a player decision.

## Decree 13 of 2026 — Kurdish citizenship

**Issued 16 January 2026.** SANA published it in Arabic and English; a
translation is on Wikisource.

**Article 1:** "Syrian Kurdish citizens are considered an essential and authentic
part of the Syrian people, and their cultural and linguistic identity is an
inseparable part of Syria's unified and diverse national identity."

Substantive provisions:
- Abolishes **all laws and exceptional measures** arising from the **1962
  Hasakah census**.
- Grants **Syrian nationality** to Kurdish-origin residents, including those
  **previously unregistered**.
- Guarantees full equality in rights and duties.
- Designates **Nowruz Day (21 March)** a **paid official holiday** throughout the
  Syrian Arab Republic.
- **Article 8:** published in the Official Gazette, in force on the date of
  issuance.

SANA frames it as ending "decades of marginalization and exclusion endured by
Syrian Kurds" and as "a balanced legal and political response" that moves the
issue "from a framework of conflict to a constitutional and legal context".

### Verdict

**VERIFIED.** This is a real and consequential decree, and it is **not** what the
fact-check report attributed to "Decree No. 13". The report's row 2.5 claimed
"Decision/Decree No. 13 of May 4, 2025: National Committee for Combating
Illicit Enrichment".

Decree numbers are **year-scoped**, so *Decree 13 of 2025* and *Decree 13 of
2026* are entirely different instruments. The report's row is wrong on subject
matter, date and body.

The **Anti-Illicit Enrichment Commission is real** — al-Suwaidan heads it, and its
settlements programme is documented in detail above — but **no founding decree
number was located**, and the report's citation for it was the absent design
document. Its legal basis in code is therefore recorded as Decree 16 plus the
commission, with no decree number.

### Game impact

**MISSING FROM GAME: the citizenship question.** Any Syrian president inherits
this as a foundational decision — a 1962-census legacy, a stateless population,
a national holiday, and a registration drive. The game has no demographic axis
beyond population totals, and no concept of citizenship or registration at all.
It is arguably the largest single unmodelled social question in the project.

## What the game gets right

Worth stating plainly, because most of this record is correction: on **every
decree the game cites — 16, 20, 98, 100, 101 — it is correct**, and where the
fact-check report described a remediation the code now matches the primary
source. The governance layer is the best-verified part of the project after the
damage and reconstruction figures.
