# Bab el-Mandeb and Hormuz maritime disruption

**Status: UNVERIFIED in this pass.** The structural claim is well-founded and
economically central; the specific premium figures are uncited.

## What the game says

Two master-deck cards, plus revenue and insurance lines:

- `event_05_bab_el_mandeb` — **scripted on turn 5**.
- `event_06_hormuz_interdiction`.
- The `Bab el-Mandeb` condition is the anchor for several agriculture and
  industry cards' cost drivers.

The strategic logic is sound and well-grounded in general terms: Syria is a net
importer of grain, fuel and fertiliser; both straits are chokepoints on its
supply routes; freight and war-risk insurance premia raise landed costs; and
Tartus and Latakia are the ports that receive the cargo.

## What the report claimed

> "Houthi missile operations in the Bab el-Mandeb strait and Iranian naval
> tensions in the Strait of Hormuz drove container shipping premiums up
> 200–300%, directly raising import delivery costs for grain, fuel, and
> fertilizers entering Tartus and Latakia ports."

Verdict `[VERIFIED FACTUAL]`.

## What could be confirmed

Nothing directly. No source for the 200–300% figure was located in this pass.

Two structural points are independently sound and worth separating from the
number:

1. **The chokepoint logic is correct.** Syria's grain, fuel and fertiliser
   imports route through or near both straits; the Bab el-Mandeb sits on the
   Red Sea route to Tartus and Latakia.
2. **Insurance premia are a distinct lever from freight rates.** The report
   conflates them in one sentence. War-risk insurance and freight are separate
   costs with separate behaviour, and the game models them as a single effect.

## Verdict

**UNVERIFIED on figures; sound on structure.** The mechanic is worth keeping. The
200–300% figure should be sourced or softened, and the freight/insurance
distinction is worth modelling separately.

## Game impact

- **UNSOURCED:** the 200–300% premium figure.
- **MISSING: the Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb de-escalation.** Red Sea shipping
  conditions have been a live variable since 2023–24, with material recovery in
  periods. A game set in 2027 that can only model *escalation* at a chokepoint
  misses half the real risk distribution. Not researched in this pass — flagged as
  a gap.
- **MISSING: separate freight and insurance costs**, which would let a player
  mitigate one without the other — rerouting, war-risk cover, alternative ports.
- **NOTE:** `event_05_bab_el_mandeb` is scripted to turn 5, which guarantees every
  playthrough sees it. That is a strong authored beat and worth keeping.
