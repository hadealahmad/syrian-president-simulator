# Assad regime collapse: 8 December 2024

**Status: VERIFIED.** The date, and the fact that it anchors the entire game's
timeline, are correct.

## What the game says

`INITIAL_YEAR = 2027`, with the game presenting a state roughly two years past
the regime's fall and ten years into reconstruction. The fact-check report lists
"Assad Regime Collapse Date: December 8, 2024" as `[VERIFIED FACTUAL]`.

The event deck and baseline flags are built around the aftermath — `as_suwayda`
autonomy, Golan tension, port-graft corruption, returnee corridors.

## What the sources say

- Assad's rule ended on **8 December 2024**; he left Damascus that morning and
  flew to Moscow. Rebel factions entered the capital on the morning of 8 December.
- Israeli forces occupied the UNDOF demilitarised buffer zone and seized Mount
  Hermon **on the same day**, immediately following Assad's fall.
- **29 January 2025** — al-Sharaa formally appointed president at the Revolution
  Victory Conference.
- The redenomination was timed for **8 December 2025**, the first anniversary of
  the ouster (per Reuters' August 2025 reporting), though it ultimately launched
  1 January 2026.
- Over **1,000,000 refugees** and **1,900,000 IDPs** have returned since
  December 2024.
- The World Bank's damage assessment period is 2011–2024, as of 31 December 2024.

## Verdict

**VERIFIED.** The date is consistently reported and the surrounding sequence
matches. The game uses it correctly as the anchor for the transitional period.

The game's 2027 start sits about two years after the collapse, which is a
defensible choice: long enough for the fiscal data to have stabilised into
something citable, short enough that the reconstruction and the political
transition are both clearly unfinished.

## Game impact

Nothing to correct. Worth noting for planning:

- The **first anniversary** is already a datable event in the game's timeline
  (turn ~4, 2027 H2) and is not modelled.
- The collapse and the Golan incursion are **the same day**. The game separates
  them across different decks and a `Golan_Tension_Escalation` flag. Bundling
  them — a single shock with a security dimension and a sovereignty dimension —
  would be closer to the historical texture and would make the Golan
  `UN_LIAISON` stance choice sharper.
- **MISSING:** the returnee wave as a first-order event. See
  [outdated-population-and-displacement.md](outdated-population-and-displacement.md).
